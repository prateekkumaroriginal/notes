What to watch

Risk
A request whose Host header is not in allowedHosts is rejected by CommonEngine.
Why
The list is derived from the tenant's environment file. New white-label sites get their hosts from the generator; a hostname added by hand anywhere else does not.
Rule
Every hostname a tenant serves must be in its environment.* URLs. Nothing else feeds the allow list.
Risk
A new component written the old way, no standalone flag, declared in an NgModule, fails to compile.
Why
It is standalone by default now, and standalone classes cannot be declared in a module.
Rule
Either write standalone: false and declare it, or leave the flag off and import it where it is used. Not both.

Nothing broke on this step. The bundler and SSR work in 17 carried the risk.
