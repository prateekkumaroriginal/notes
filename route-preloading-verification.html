What the labels mean

The source column tells you what we found in the code. The browser column tells you what we actually tested.

LabelPlain meaning
Source verifiedPreloading is wired in the code. This alone does not prove it works.
Observed, cached flowIt worked in the browser, using files already saved in its cache. A first-time download was not tested.
InconclusiveWe could not confirm whether that control is wired correctly.
BlockedWe could not test it, usually because login, permissions or test data were missing, or the control was unavailable.
PartialWe tested some of the listed controls or steps, but not all.
Checklist mismatchThe checklist describes the wrong trigger, destination or scope.
Missing bindingThat control has no preloading wired up.

For the feed-video row, preloading worked before clicking. It used cached files; a completely empty browser cache was not tested.
