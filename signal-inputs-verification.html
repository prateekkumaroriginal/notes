Fixes after the schematic

The first QA build had about 500 errors. Most of that was the schematic being sloppy with types and with this., not a surprise on a repo this size. The ones that actually mattered:

What brokeWhat I did
viewChild('foo') with no type Hundreds of originals had no annotation, so the signal came back as unknown and openModal() failed. Restored types from git, or any when there was nothing to restore.
ngx-bootstrap initialState on AlertModal Object.assign would have overwritten the signal functions with strings. I put those inputs and the EventEmitter outputs back. This modal is opened that way from a lot of places.
Portfolio song / podcast / video parents this.step3Component.postPriceComponent.ppForm became a mess of locals pointing at the wrong object. Wired them back through step3Component()?.postPriceComponent().
private readonly screenClick = output() Angular rejects a private output. Dropped private on video-player-v2. That one took the whole player out of every standalone import list until it compiled.
header-layout brandClick.observed output() has no .observed. Kept the EventEmitter so a logo click still goes home when the parent is not listening.

What stayed on decorators

  • @Input() set … setters, and fields written from TypeScript or ngOnChanges.
  • Required inputs the schematic could not type.
  • @ViewChild setters such as replyCommentInput on post detail, home, forum, and profile.
  • Queries initialized with = null. The signal API does not allow that write.
  • The 10 leftover outputs. Alert modal and a couple of others still get .subscribe() or .pipe() on the EventEmitter.

Do not force those. The next person who wants 100% coverage needs to change the call sites first.
