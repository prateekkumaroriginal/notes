Risks

  • Removing something from SharedModule without adding it at the use site. The build fails. That is the point. Fix the import, do not put it back in shared.
  • Video player @defer with no standalone import. Size looks good, player never boots.
  • Idle phone fields on a busy main thread. Short blank, then the input. Accept it or switch those sites to on immediate if QA hates the hole.
  • Prerendered HTML will not contain deferred blocks. Phone was already client-only via cs.isBrowser. Videos and emoji become client-only too. Fine for those widgets. Do not @defer SEO text.
  • Header marketing data becomes async. Possible empty mega-menu for one tick, and an extra SSR change-detection pass.
