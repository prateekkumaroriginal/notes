Why step 2 became two commits

The plan counted about 22 SharedModule pieces for this step. That number only covered the marketing layouts. The page behind / also renders ProfileComponent on white-label sites and SearchedPostListComponent on the search page, and those two pull in the post card and everything around it. Converting both brought the step to 54 SharedModule pieces plus the 55 marketing components. I split it so the marketing pages and the home page can be reviewed apart.
